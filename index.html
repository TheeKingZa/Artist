Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to elevate the Vaal community through music and to create transformative partnerships that drive cultural and social progress.

  • Empower the Vaal Community — Amplifying local voices and identity.
  • Catalyze Cultural Change — Inspiring dialogue and positive progress.
  • Forge Strategic Collaborations — Partnering with brands that value authenticity.

About TMBTM

THEYMUSTBETHEMOBB (TMBT) is a vibrant music collective born in the heart of Vaal. Built on authenticity and cultural pride, the movement exists to amplify local voices and transform music into a force for unity, empowerment, and social change.

More than entertainers, we are community ambassadors. Through music, live events, and strategic collaborations, TMBT drives cultural energy while creating opportunities for emerging talent and forward-thinking brands.

Our Artists

Meet the voices behind the movement.

HEMUSTBEFRWSH

Hip Hop / Trap

Tshiamo Lehlohonolo Chabane, known as HeMustBeFrwshGod, is a Sebokeng-born rapper blending lyrical depth, cultural pride, and resilient storytelling into a sound rooted in authenticity, growth, and the undeniable spirit of Vaal.

HEMUSTBESWAE

Hip Hop / Trap

Andile Ramaidi, known as HeMustBeSwae, is a Sebokeng-born Hip Hop artist blending sharp lyricism, confident delivery, and modern trap influence into a sound driven by ambition, hustle, and the evolving energy of Vaal.
